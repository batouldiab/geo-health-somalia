Reconciling population

WorldPop 2020 gives about 441,000 people for Bakool's squares; UNFPA PESS 2014 counted 367,226 and the FSNAU/IPC projection for late 2024 is 543,371. The grid keeps WorldPop as the spatial pattern and shows the bracket beside every district total, so no square inherits a precision the sources do not have.

Disaggregation, done honestly

A national rate (maternal deaths per birth, under-5 deaths per child, health workers per 10,000) spread over the squares in proportion to the people in the relevant age-and-sex band gives an expected count per square, not a measurement. It is useful for ranking where a mobile team reaches the most girls or children, and it is labelled a model estimate wherever it appears. The age-and-sex bands come from WorldPop's constrained 2015–2030 rasters (CC BY 4.0).

Where the doctors are

No public dataset places individual doctors or nurses in Somalia. The proposed SMS line creates that registry as a by-product: a sender registers a facility and a position once, so every later question carries a lat/lon without anyone mapping staff by hand. It is operational data, logged with consent, never scraped.

Access to light, roads and paths

VIIRS radiance per square and the HDX-OSM road lengths by class (trunk to path) live on the NexaSEE platform. For the health question they add what the travel-time surface lacks: whether a populated square has any grid power or night activity, and whether the "road" to care is a trunk road or a footpath, which decides whether a vehicle referral is even possible.
